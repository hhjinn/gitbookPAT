# Account Creation Request

Users without an OwlDB account can request one from the **Login** page.

1. On the **Login** page, click **Request Account Creation**.
2. Enter your ID, name, password, password confirmation, and email address.
3. Click **Request**.
4. In the account creation request modal, click **Confirm**.
5. When you click **Confirm**, you are taken to the **Login** page.

### Account Approval

When the administrator approves the account creation, the account is activated.
