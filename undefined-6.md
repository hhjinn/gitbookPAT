OwlDB provides notifications for various events that occur while using it. To view notifications, click the 🔔 icon in the top-right corner of the console screen.

> 📷 **[이미지]** 이미지

- Click the ✔ icon on the right side of a message to change its read status.
- Messages marked as read are automatically deleted after 30 days.
- Top-right corner **Meatball menu** icon > **Mark all as read**Clicking this marks all messages as read.
