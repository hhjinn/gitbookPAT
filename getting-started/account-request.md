Users who do not have an OwlDB account can **Login** request account creation directly on the page.

1. **Login** On the page, **Request Account Creation** Click the button.
2. Enter the information below. ID Name Password Confirm Password Email
3. **Request** Click the button.
4. In the account creation request modal, **Confirm** Click the button.
5. **Confirm** when you click the button, **Login** Move to the page.

## Account Approval

When the administrator approves the account creation, the account is activated.
