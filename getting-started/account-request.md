Users without an OwlDB account can request account creation directly on the **Login** page.

1. Click the **Request Account Creation** button on the **Login** page.
2. Enter the information below. ID Name Password Confirm Password Email
3. Click the **Request** button.
4. Click the **Confirm** button in the account creation request modal.
5. When you click the **Confirm** button, you are redirected to the **Login** page.

## Account Approval <a href="#account-approval" id="account-approval"></a>

Once the administrator approves the account creation, the account is activated.
