When a new version of OwlDB is released, you can apply the latest version directly from the console.

1. A sky-blue update notification banner appears **at the top of the dashboard**.
2. When you click the **Update** button in the notification banner, the update proceeds.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note**

Service updates do not affect the databases in operation.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note**

If you have closed the notification banner, you can proceed with the service update by clicking the **info icon** at the bottom of the detailed menu area.
{% endhint %}
